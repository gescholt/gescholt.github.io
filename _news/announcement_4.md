New release: [signrules](https://github.com/gescholt/signrules) 0.5.3, now on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/ecological_sign_rules/) as `ecological_sign_rules`. It proves Lotka–Volterra sign patterns impossible, writes a machine certificate for every proof, and replays each certificate with an independent checker. It implements the sign-completion algorithm of [Strata of Ecological Coexistence via Grassmannians](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-026-00837-7).
